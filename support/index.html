Support

Something not working, or an idea for Water? Write to 88sagacom@gmail.com. We usually answer within a day.

How do I add a friend?

Sign in with Apple, open Friends on the Today screen and share your invite link. When your friend opens it and joins, you see each other's islands. Swipe left on Today to visit them.

What is a splash?

A friendly nudge to drink water. You can splash each friend once every 3 hours. If they allowed notifications, they get one.

How do I earn water points?

Every day you reach your goal earns water points, and long streaks earn a bonus. Tap the water points at the top of Today to try islands and colors for free and get the ones you like.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and find Water. There is a Water widget in several sizes, and a Best Buddy widget that shows you and your best friend side by side.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for Water in the iPhone Settings app, and pick a reminder level in Water's Settings.

How do I block or report someone?

In Friends, touch and hold the friend and choose Block and report, then pick a reason. They are removed and cannot add you again. Reports go to our team and are reviewed within 24 hours. To part ways quietly, choose Remove friend.

How do I delete my account?

In Water, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Your account and synced data are deleted from our server at once. See the privacy policy for details.
